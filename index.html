For Suppliers

Restaurants, hotels, caterers, butcheries and food businesses can contact NCS when they have fats or used cooking oil available for collection. This is one of the most important indicators because building a reliable supplier network is essential to the proposed business model.

For Industrial Customers

On the other side of the business, Industrial customers may include manufacturers that are involved in industries such as soap production, biodiesel, cosmetics, chemicals, and other processes where suitable fats and oils can be used as raw materials.

Professional Service

The business will focus on reliable collection, safe transportation, professional customer service, and effective communication with suppliers and industrial customers. This approach can create business value by turning potentially unwanted materials into resources that may have further commercial use.

Why this website is needed

NCS is still a proposed business, so it needs one clear online place where suppliers and customers can understand the business. The website helps the company build trust, explain its process and collect useful enquiries.

Main website goal

To create a professional and user-friendly online presence that introduces the business, explains collection and transport services, and makes communication easier for suppliers and industrial customers.

Cleaner supply-chain communication

The design now gives visitors a clear route from learning about the business to requesting collection, asking for a quote or contacting NCS directly.
