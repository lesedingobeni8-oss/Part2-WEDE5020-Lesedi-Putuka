Supplier Collection Request

This page is for businesses that have used cooking oil or fats available for collection. This will allow businesses that have fats or used cooking oil available to submit their details online. The form can request information such as the business name, contact person, telephone number, email address, material type, estimated quantity, collection location, preferred collection date, and additional information. This feature will make it easier for Ngobeni Chain Supply (NCS) to receive and organise potential collection requests.

Who can use this service?

Potential suppliers include restaurants, hotels, catering businesses, food-processing companies and butcheries. The goal is to make it easier for these businesses to contact NCS instead of relying only on informal communication.
