About Us

Ngobeni Chain Supply is a proposed South African business that focuses on collecting and transporting used cooking oil and animal fats from suppliers to businesses that can use them.

Who we are

NCS works mainly in the business-to-business market. The company aims to connect suppliers such as restaurants, hotels, caterers, food processors and butcheries with industrial customers who need suitable fats and oils.

The business is not only about waste removal. It is about creating a more organised supply chain where unwanted materials can become useful resources for other businesses.

Mission

To provide reliable collection and transportation services for fats and used cooking oil while giving business customers clear communication and professional service.

Vision

To become a trusted supply-chain partner for businesses that produce or use recovered fats and oils in South Africa.

Values

Reliability, safety, honesty, environmental responsibility and good customer service guide the way the business wants to operate.
