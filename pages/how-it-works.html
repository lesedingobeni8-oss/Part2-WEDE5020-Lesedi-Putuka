How It Works

The process is designed to be easy for suppliers and clear for industrial customers.

1. Supplier sends a request

The supplier gives business details, location, material type, quantity and a preferred collection date.

2. NCS checks the request

The business reviews the details and confirms if the material and location can be collected.

3. Collection is arranged

A collection date and transport plan are confirmed with the supplier.

4. Material is transported

The used cooking oil or fat is moved safely to the next suitable business point.

5. Industrial customer enquiry is handled

Manufacturers or other business customers can enquire about available material and transport arrangements.
